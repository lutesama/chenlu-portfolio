Art

艺术

Essays on exhibitions, institutions, and the art world as a system — how artworks circulate, acquire value, and shape ways of seeing.

All essays are written from a reporting perspective, based on field research and institutional observation.

围绕展览、艺术机构与艺术世界的系统运作——观察作品如何流通、如何获得价值，并如何塑造我们的观看方式。

所有文章均基于采访、田野观察或机构研究完成。

What makes an artwork worth a sky-high price? 什么塑造了艺术品的天价？
2019·Reported Feature·Market / Auctions
2019·报道型特写·市场 / 拍卖
From auctions, galleries, and collectors, this essay examines how artworks acquire value — and why price often has little to do with art itself. 从拍卖、画廊与藏家出发，分析艺术品价值如何被建构——以及为什么价格常常与“艺术本身”关系不大。

From photography auctions and galleries, this essay explores how value is constructed in a medium once defined by reproducibility. 从摄影拍卖与画廊体系出发，讨论一种曾以“可复制性”为特征的媒介，其价值如何被建构。

Back to photography through “anti-photography” 在“反摄影”中回归摄影本质

From the Arles photography festival, this essay reflects on how photography circulates between exhibition, industry, and public imagination. 以集美·阿尔勒摄影节为例，观察摄影如何在展览机制、产业系统与公众想象之间流通。

“The Uffizi for everyone”: what renovation changes, and what it costs “乌菲兹美术馆属于所有人”
