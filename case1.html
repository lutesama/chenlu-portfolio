Xiao Zhan Incident: Turning Explanation into Participation

肖战事件：把“解释”做成“参与”

A cross-media editorial package that moved from written explainers to scholar interviews, audience question-collecting, and video explainers — designed as a system, not a single article.

一个跨媒体的编辑传播系统：从“解释稿”建立共识，到学者访谈提供框架，再到新媒体征集问题、视频答疑与知识点拆条，把热点转译成可理解、可参与、可传播的内容链路。

Cross-media 跨媒体 Editorial system 编辑系统 · Role: Editor / Producer (planning · writing · distribution) 角色：策划编辑 / 统筹（选题 · 写作 · 分发联动）

Context

背景

When a public controversy accelerates online, audiences often lack shared vocabulary and interpretive frameworks. The goal was to build a calm, research-based explanation layer — then convert it into participation and repeatable distribution units.

热点加速时，公众往往缺少共同语言与解释框架。这个专题的目标是：先建立可理解的“解释层”，再把解释转化为参与与可复用的传播单元，让复杂议题变得可讨论、可延展。

System design

系统设计

01

Explainer

解释稿

Define vocabulary and baseline knowledge (e.g., what fan fiction / fandom culture is).

先解释基本概念与语境（如“同人文化是什么”），建立共同语言。

02

Scholar interview

学者访谈

Add an interpretive framework: why fans mobilize, how communities form, and what “madness” means structurally.

通过学者提供解释框架：粉丝为何会“疯狂”、社群机制如何运作。

03

Question collecting

征集问题

Co-design with the audience: collect questions on social channels and convert them into a structured FAQ.

新媒体联动征集问题，把读者的困惑“结构化”，形成 Q&A 目录。

04

Video dialogue

视频对话

A second interview in video format to answer audience questions — increasing trust and retention.

与学者二次视频对话，围绕征集问题答疑，增强参与感与留存。

05

Modular explainers

知识点拆条

Translate key concepts into short video units for multi-platform distribution.

把文章里的关键知识点拆成短视频/卡片式内容，用于多平台传播。

06

Distribution loop

分发闭环

Articles → social posts → questions → video → short units → back-links — forming an editorial flywheel.

文章→社媒→征集→视频→拆条→回流文章，形成编辑飞轮。

What this demonstrates

这个案例证明了什么

I build editorial systems that convert complexity into shared language, then translate that language into participation and distribution: writing → interaction → video → modular units → feedback loop.

我擅长把复杂议题转译成共同语言，并把“解释”进一步转化为“参与 + 分发”的系统：写作 → 互动 → 视频 → 拆条 → 回流闭环。
