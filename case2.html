Xiao Zhan Incident: Turning Explanation into Participation

肖战事件：把“解释”做成“参与”

A cross-media editorial response to the 2020 Xiao Zhan fan controversy, triggered by mass reporting against fan fiction platforms (including AO3), which escalated into a nationwide debate on fan culture, creative freedom, and platform governance. The project reframed a fast-moving fandom conflict into a broader public discussion by combining explainers, scholar interviews, audience question-collecting, and video dialogues — designed as an editorial system rather than a single article.

这是一次针对 2020 年“肖战粉丝事件”的跨媒体编辑实践。事件由粉丝对同人作品及 AO3 等平台的集中举报引发，迅速升级为关于粉丝文化、创作边界与平台治理的公共讨论。本项目通过解释稿、学者访谈、问题征集与视频对话，把一场高度情绪化的粉圈冲突，转译为可理解、可参与、可持续讨论的文化议题。

Cross-media 跨媒体 · Editorial system 编辑系统 · Editorial system designer / Editor / Producer (planning · writing · cross-platform coordination) 角色：编辑系统设计 / 策划编辑 / 统筹（选题 · 写作 · 跨平台联动）

Context

背景

In February 2020, mass reporting by fan groups led to the blocking of AO3 in mainland China, turning an internal fandom conflict into a public cultural shock. As unfamiliar terms such as fan fiction, real-person fiction, and participatory culture entered mainstream discourse, the absence of shared vocabulary amplified misunderstanding and moral panic. The editorial goal was to first stabilize the discussion through explanation, then gradually convert explanation into participation.

2020 年 2 月，粉丝群体的集中举报导致AO3在中国大陆无法访问，一场原本发生在粉圈内部的冲突迅速演变为公共文化事件。“同人创作”“真人同人”“参与式文化”等概念第一次大规模进入公众视野，但缺乏基本解释，情绪先于理解扩散。这个专题的目标，是先建立解释层，再把解释转化为可参与的公共讨论。

System design

系统设计

01

Explainer

解释稿

Define baseline concepts — what fan fiction is, how it differs from piracy, and where legal and moral boundaries are debated.

先解释基本概念与语境（如“同人文化是什么”），建立共同语言。

02

Scholar interview

学者访谈

Introduce an academic framework through Henry Jenkins, connecting the incident to participatory culture, fan labor, and platform power.

通过学者提供解释框架：粉丝为何会“疯狂”、社群机制如何运作。

03

Question collecting

征集问题

Co-design with the audience: collect questions on social channels and convert them into a structured FAQ.

新媒体联动征集问题，把读者的困惑“结构化”，形成 Q&A 目录。

04

Video dialogue

视频对话

A second interview in video format to answer audience questions — increasing trust and retention.

与学者二次视频对话，围绕征集问题答疑，增强参与感与留存。

05

Modular explainers

知识点拆条

Translate key concepts into short video units for multi-platform distribution.

把文章里的关键知识点拆成短视频/卡片式内容，用于多平台传播。

06

Distribution loop

分发闭环

Articles → social posts → questions → video → short units → back-links — forming an editorial flywheel.

文章→社媒→征集→视频→拆条→回流文章，形成编辑飞轮。

What this demonstrates

这个案例证明了什么

This structure later became a reusable template for handling fast-moving public controversies. I build editorial systems that convert complexity into shared language, then translate that language into participation and distribution: writing → interaction → video → modular units → feedback loop. The case was later referenced in university communication courses as a teaching example, showing how media explanation can extend beyond news cycles into academic and educational contexts.

我擅长把复杂议题转译成共同语言，并把“解释”进一步转化为“参与 + 分发”的系统：写作 → 互动 → 视频 → 拆条 → 回流闭环。该案例后来被高校传播学课程作为教学案例引用，展示了媒体解释如何超出新闻周期，进入学术与教育语境。
