Museum Launch: Turning “Play” into Public Conversation

儿童博物馆项目：把“玩”变成公共讨论

A hybrid launch campaign that combined a pre-launch H5 experience, short video messages from public figures, and an on-site opening program — building attention online, then converting it into real-world participation.

一个线上线下联动的发布传播：以 H5 预热与名人短视频建立议题热度，再通过开幕活动与教育论坛把关注带到现场，让“儿童、玩与教育”成为可参与的公共讨论。

Campaign 传播项目 Cultural institution 文化机构 Online → Offline 线上→线下 · Role: Project support (content planning · coordination · materials) 角色：项目支持（内容策划协助 · 统筹协调 · 物料支持）

Context

背景

For a museum launch, the hardest part is not “making noise,” but making the mission legible: what is being built, why it matters, and how the public can take part. The campaign needed a clear narrative spine — then multiple formats to carry it across channels.

博物馆项目的发布，难点不只是“做热闹”，而是让使命变得可理解：它要解决什么问题、为什么值得关注、公众如何参与。 因此我们需要先把叙事骨架立住，再用多种内容形态把它带到不同渠道。

System design

系统设计

01

Narrative spine

叙事主线

Define one clear message: play is a serious part of learning, not a distraction.

先确立一句话主张：玩不是分心，而是学习的一部分。

02

Pre-launch H5

H5 预热

Use a lightweight interactive page to make the idea easy to share and remember.

用轻量互动页面把概念“变简单、可转发、可记住”。

03

Short video messages

名人短视频

Invite public voices to speak in everyday language — shifting the tone from “institutional” to “human.”

邀请公众人物用更生活化的语言发声，把“机构表达”转成“人的表达”。

04

Opening program

开幕活动

Design an on-site experience that translates the narrative into real participation.

把主张落到现场体验：让观众“看见、参与、被触发”。

05

Education forum

教育论坛

Connect practitioners across regions to extend the project from an event into a network.

组织教育从业者交流，把项目从“一次发布”延展为“一个网络”。

06

Cross-channel loop

跨渠道闭环

H5 → video → event → forum → recap materials, with back-links to reinforce the narrative.

H5 → 视频 → 活动 → 论坛 → 复盘物料，形成可回流的叙事链路。

What this demonstrates

这个案例证明了什么

I can translate abstract mission statements into a clear narrative spine, then ship that spine through multiple formats — interactive, video, events, and convenings — to make cultural ideas accessible and actionable.

我能把抽象的“使命表达”翻译成清晰的叙事主线，再用多种内容形态把它落地——互动页面、短视频、活动与论坛——让文化理念变得可理解、可参与、可行动。
