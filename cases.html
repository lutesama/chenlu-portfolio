From butterfly watching to outdoor lifestyle narrative: building a content system around curiosity, place, and movement 从观蝶到户外生活方式：围绕好奇心、地点与行走建立内容体系
Case· Content strategy· Community & growth· Video storytelling
案例· 内容策略· 社群与增长· 视频叙事
Starting from butterfly observation, this project reframes nature watching as a lifestyle practice — connecting movement, landscape, and everyday curiosity. Rather than species-based science content, it focuses on experience, place, and narrative rhythm. The project explored how niche knowledge can scale into outdoor, travel, and lifestyle contexts, leading to brand collaborations, publishing projects, and platform-level exposure. 这个项目从观蝶出发，把自然观察重新理解为一种生活方式——与行走、风景和日常好奇心相连。 它不以物种知识为中心，而是强调体验、地点与叙事节奏。 项目探索了小众兴趣如何延展为户外、旅行与生活方式内容，并由此促成品牌合作、出版项目与平台级传播。

From a trending figure to a cross-media editorial system 从热点人物到跨媒体编辑系统
Case· Cross-media storytelling· Editorial system· Public engagement
案例· 跨媒体叙事· 编辑系统· 公共参与
This case demonstrates how a single cultural moment can be developed into a structured content system. Through concept explanation, in-depth interviews, reader-driven Q&A, and short-form video translation, the project tested how editorial thinking can scale across platforms while maintaining depth and coherence. 这个案例展示了如何将一次文化热点转化为结构化的内容系统。 通过概念解释、深度采访、读者问答共创与短视频转译，验证了编辑思维在多平台传播中的可扩展性与深度。
03 03
From public education to cultural engagement: building a cross-media launch for a children’s museum 从公共教育到文化参与：儿童博物馆的跨媒体启动项目
Case· Cultural institution· Public engagement· Event & content strategy
案例· 文化机构· 公共参与· 活动与内容策划
As part of the launch of the Lao Niu Children’s Museum project at the BNU Philanthropy Research Institute, this case explored how public education themes can be translated into cultural participation. The project combined an online H5 pre-launch campaign — featuring short video contributions from public figures such as Yu Minhong, Yao Ming, and Li Yinuo on play and childhood education — with an offline opening event and an international forum co-hosted with the Hawaii Children’s Museum, bringing together museum educators from across China. 在北京师范大学公益研究院参与老牛儿童博物馆项目启动过程中，该案例探索了如何将公共教育议题转化为可参与的文化实践。 项目前期通过 H5 线上内容预热，邀请俞敏洪、姚明、李一诺等公众人物录制短视频，讨论“玩”与儿童教育的关系；线下则策划了开幕之夜活动，并与夏威夷儿童博物馆联合举办国际教育论坛，汇集全国博物馆教育从业者参与。
