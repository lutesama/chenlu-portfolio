Notes

说明

Fragrance has no body. This series observes how presence can exist without form, without narrative, and without a subject. What is photographed is not the scent, but the space it quietly inhabits, and imagined closeness.

香水没有身体。这组作品观察一种没有形态、没有叙事、也没有主体的存在方式。被拍摄的不是香味本身，而是它安静占据的空间，与被想象的亲近。
