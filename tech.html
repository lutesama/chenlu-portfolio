Tech

科技

Writing on technology as a system of thought and action— from theory and technical design to deployment, governance, and lived experience. My work translates complex technical debates into public-facing frameworks, focusing on risk, accountability, and how emerging technologies move from laboratories into real-world contexts.

把技术视为一种思想与行动的系统： 从理论与技术设计，到现实部署、治理结构与人的经验。 我的写作致力于将复杂的技术议题转译为公众可理解的框架， 关注风险、责任，以及新技术如何从实验室进入现实世界。

EDITOR’S SELECTION Selected tech writing — explainers, interviews, and applied features.
